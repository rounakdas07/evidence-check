What this tool does

  1. Load a batch. Search PubMed records, enter IDs, or import your own abstracts.
  2. Check the details. The tool spots topic terms, experimental-setting clues and a small list of compound names. It selects a result-like sentence from each abstract.
  3. Make the decision. Keep, Maybe or Skip. Correct the labels and add notes where useful.
  4. Download the table. The source link and original selected sentence travel with each row.

How the suggestions work

These are transparent keyword rules, not an AI judgement of scientific quality. A mention of a compound does not mean it was tested. Human or animal terms can describe background rather than the actual experiment. Check the supplied text.

The sentence picker favours phrases such as “we found” and “our results”. It copies an existing sentence; it does not generate a summary or determine whether the finding is positive. You can select a different sentence from the abstract.

What it does not do

It does not read full papers or PDFs, establish efficacy, assess risk of bias, or complete a systematic review. Missing information stays missing. You make all inclusion decisions.

Sources and privacy

Live search uses the Europe PMC API. The bundled example contains eight abstracts marked CC BY in Europe PMC, with authors and source links preserved. The publication remains the source of truth for its licence and scientific content.

Pasted text, uploads and notes are processed locally and saved to this browser where storage is available. Search queries and PubMed IDs are sent to Europe PMC; your local notes and uploads are not. No account, API key or external AI service is required.
