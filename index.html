What the three labels mean
Supported
Every material part follows from the supplied summary.
Contradicted
The summary explicitly conflicts with part of the claim.
Not established
The summary is insufficient. An unsupported claim is not automatically false.

    
How the dataset was made
Six primary-study abstracts were read to create concise, original evidence paraphrases. Each has one supported claim, one contradiction and one overreach. All 18 claims are constructed tests, not quotations or captured model outputs. Cases were prepared with AI assistance; reference labels remain provisional pending independent scientific review.
The task is entailment against the supplied paraphrase, not extraction from the full paper. Each paper forms a group of three related cases: split by paper, not individual case, if adapting this for training.

    
What a score can—and cannot—tell you
Agreement measures exact label matches among submitted cases. Coverage is reported separately. Macro-F1 is available only with all 18 predictions. “Unsupported claims accepted” counts contradicted or not-established reference claims labelled supported.
This tiny, deliberately balanced set has no statistical power to certify reliability. Sources are familiar historical papers, so model training contamination is possible. It is not a systematic review, current prescribing resource, clinical safety score or independently validated benchmark.
