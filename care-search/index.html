About this directory

A searchable view of CQC’s public register, built to help research care providers and their registered locations.

What is included?

We include locations with at least one of six CQC service types: nursing homes, residential homes, homecare agencies, supported living, supported housing or shared lives. Other services are excluded.

What does “provider” mean?

One CQC provider ID. This is a registered legal entity, not necessarily an entire corporate group. Separate registrations are not merged, even if they share a brand or website.

What do the counts mean?

Unique CQC location IDs in the selected care service types. Size filters use each provider’s total across the dataset; region and service filters then find matching locations. Locations may be branches or offices, not individual homes. Dual registrations can refer to the same physical premises. Counts are not staffing, occupancy or market-capacity estimates.

Dates and limitations

The directory is a fixed 19 November 2026 snapshot. Provider websites, brand labels, company numbers and care-home bed counts are joined by CQC IDs from the 2 November 2026 file. Missing values are left blank. Beds are shown per location and never summed across dual registrations.

CQC reports delays in its published data, including registration cancellations. Verify a record with CQC and the provider before acting. The directory does not indicate buying intent, vacancies or whether a provider already uses Sona.
