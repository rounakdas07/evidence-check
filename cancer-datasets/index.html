Know what you’re looking at.

A curated starting point for finding cancer research data, with original links, access notes and practical limitations. It includes individual cohorts, larger collections and laboratory screens.

What the labels mean

Patients
Human tumour or patient cohort data. Check the record for which clinical outcomes are actually available.
Cell lines
Experiments on cultured cells. Drug sensitivity or viability is not patient response.
Mixed
Several materials or access tiers. Read the details before selecting a resource.

Access is specific to each data layer

Open means the described data can be accessed publicly; registration or acceptance of terms may still be required. Mixed means access differs between layers or included datasets. Controlled means an application or permission is required for the described data. Open access does not automatically permit every commercial use. Follow the original licence and consent conditions.

Search behaviour

Words are matched across the name, cancer, treatments, data types and description. Every search word must match. Filters combine together. A cancer filter includes resources marked “Pan-cancer”, so broad collections remain visible; check their coverage in the original source. Quick searches apply their stated preset alongside any filters you add.

Coverage and limits

This is a fixed, curated directory, not a live feed or a systematic review. Counts are resource entries, not independent patient cohorts; collections can contain studies listed separately. “Not listed” does not establish that an outcome or data type is absent. Publication year refers to the linked resource or study, not its latest update. A successful source check confirms the public description, not every underlying file or eligibility for access.
